# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want this project to stay respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Contributors should:

- communicate clearly and kindly
- assume good intent
- focus feedback on the work, not the person
- help keep the project safe for questions, reviews, and iteration

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment or intimidation
- discriminatory language or personal attacks
- deliberate disclosure of private or sensitive information
- hostile review behavior that blocks collaboration

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior harms contributors or puts the project at risk.
